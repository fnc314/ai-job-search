## `roadmap:edit`

Open the interactive roadmap manager (Kotlin TUI). Use ↑/↓ to navigate, Enter to inspect.


- **Usage:** `roadmap:edit [subcommand] [item-id]`

### Arguments
- **`[subcommand]`** — i or e to jump to a specific item

  **Choices:** `i`, `e`
- **`[item-id]`** — Roadmap item ID (e.g., RM-001)
