## `secrets:decrypt`

Decrypt credentials.tar.gz.enc into frontend/apps/android and frontend/apps/ios/iosApp


- **Usage:** `secrets:decrypt [-p --passphrase <passphrase>]`

### Flags
- **`-p --passphrase <passphrase>`** — Passphrase for symmetric decryption (defaults to SECRETS_PASSPHRASE env var or interactive prompt)
