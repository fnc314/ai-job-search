Job Summary
As Senior Engineering Manager, Device Products & Operations, you will oversee software engineering across PatientPoint's device-side product areas - field operations and device management tooling, exam-room patient engagement technology, waiting-room displays, and back-office systems - all running on Android and BrightSign devices in clinical and on-site settings.

You will be the Engineering Manager for a mix of senior Engineering Leads and individual-contributor engineers, owning hiring, performance, and career growth directly, and stepping into the Engineering Lead's technical-direction role yourself where a team needs it.

This is a hands-on role: you'll stay close to the code, contribute directly where it matters, champion AI-assisted and agentic ways of working across your teams, and bring credible, current expertise across Android (including Custom Android ROM, kiosk/rooted device management), AWS (IoT Core, Lambda, Bedrock AgentCore), and JavaScript/React front-ends to the technical decisions your teams make day to day.

What You'll Do
Own engineering outcomes across PatientPoint's device product areas - field operations tooling, exam-room technology, waiting-room displays, and back-office systems.
Serve as the Engineering Manager for a mix of senior Engineering Leads and individual-contributor engineers - owning hiring, performance, and career growth directly - and step into the Engineering Lead's technical-direction role yourself where a team needs it.
Stay close to the code: lead code reviews, contribute directly where needed, and guide teams on technical best practices.
Champion AI-assisted and agentic engineering practices - in your own work, across your teams' day-to-day development, and in the AI-powered capabilities we build into our products (e.g., automated device diagnostics).
Coordinate priorities and dependencies across three or more concurrently active product areas with different tech stacks and team maturity.
Contribute to cross-org architecture discussions (ARB/RFCs), bringing a device-fleet perspective to architecture trade-off conversations.
Help engineers and Engineering Leads grow in their roles and career paths at PatientPoint.
Partner closely with Product and Design leads on execution across the device product areas.
Manage a blended team of full-time and contract engineers, onshore and offshore.
Escalate organizational, staffing, and resourcing needs for the device product areas to engineering leadership.
