# Job Evaluation Framework

## Core Skill Match Areas
- **Strong Match:** Android Development (Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, KMP), Mobile Architecture, Gradle Build Systems, DevEx Automation (Bash), Technical Leadership.
- **Moderate Match:** Full-Stack/Backend (React Native, Firebase, GCP), Web Development (JS/TS), Security/Authentication Integrations.
- **Weak Match:** Roles exclusively focused on iOS (Swift/Objective-C), embedded systems, or legacy data engineering.

## Career Goals & Motivation Filters
- **Ideal Role:** Principal Android Engineer, Staff Mobile Engineer, or Mobile Architect focusing on large-scale platforms, developer experience (DevEx), and technical strategy.
- **Target Environments:** FinTech/FinServ, Organizations undergoing modernization, and Non-Tech sectors where technical leadership can drive significant business value and "stand out."
- **Key Motivators:** Reducing technical debt, standardizing architecture, mentoring engineering teams, and improving developer tooling/workflows.
- **Location & Salary:** 
  - **Ideal:** Remote or Pittsburgh-based.
  - **Acceptable (Super-commute):** New York City (NYC) or Washington D.C. (DC) for $200k+ roles.
  - **Borderline:** San Francisco (SF) or Los Angeles (LA) relocation if compensation maintains purchasing power parity ($200k+).
  - **Stretch:** Philadelphia.
  - **Confirmed 2026-10-30:** willing to relocate to New York City or Los Angeles, and willing to have a level conversation where the posted req sits below his experience. The $200k+ figure above is the target, not a precondition for applying: he chose to apply to DoorDash's Developer Experience Android req at a $130.6k-$192k national band with the ceiling flagged up front. Comp gets resolved in the recruiter screen, not in the cover letter.
- **Deal-breakers:** Consulting roles, hybrid application tech stacks (React Native/Flutter) as the primary focus, lack of architectural agency, highly siloed environments, and pure People Management roles (target is Technical Leadership).

## Calibration from Past Applications
- **High-Comp Relocation Signal:** High-comp roles in SF/NYC (OpenAI, Disney) are strong fits despite relocation requirements, provided base compensation clears the $200k threshold to maintain purchasing power parity with Pittsburgh.
- **Sweet Spot:** Roles with "Staff," "Principal," or "Lead Architect" titles (The Trade Desk, Robinhood, Waymo, OpenAI) consistently score 90+ based on the requirement for systemic impact and technical strategy.
- **Exclusion Signal:** Be cautious with "Founding" or "Full Stack" roles where iOS or deep non-GCP/Firebase backend is a primary requirement (e.g., SentiLink rejection).
- **Conversion Reality Check:** Be wary of high fit scores; as of Oct 2026, very few applications have moved to interview stages despite strong technical alignment. Scoring should prioritize "Grounding" and "Surgical Fit" over broad technical matches.

### Updated 2026-10-30 (derived from 81 tracker rows and 79 archived outcomes)

- **Fit score does not predict conversion.** Rejected applications average 82.2 fit (n=19) while interview-stage applications average 77.5 (n=4). Nine rejections scored 88 or higher and produced zero interviews. A score above 90 must no longer be read as "likely to advance"; it means "the resume matches the posting," nothing more.
- **Reddit is a closed door: 0 of 4.** Staff Android Platform (98), Senior Design System (94), Consumer Engineering (89), and Brand Ad Formats (open). Four attempts, highest scores in the dataset, no screen. Cold applications convert elsewhere, so the implication here is specific to Reddit: either find a referral or direct recruiter contact, or stop spending applications on them.
- **Brand-tier targets convert at 0% so far.** OpenAI 0/2 (96, 90), Coinbase 0/2 (88, 88), Capital One 0/1 (95). Keep applying selectively, but do not count them as pipeline; their value today is calibration signal, not expected conversion.
- **Automotive and embedded Android are uncalibrated, not strong.** General Motors (98 and 98) and Scout Motors (98) are the three highest-scoring open applications and have produced zero recruiter contact. Treat high fit on an unproven segment as unknown until a recruiter actually engages.
- **An ATS can misreport its own process, and that can kill an application (Capital One).** Capital One's system reported a required assessment as outstanding, then removed the application from consideration for not completing it, when no assessment had ever been issued. The back-and-forth with their recruiting team was real and unresolved. Lesson: when a portal or automated email claims a required step is pending, escalate in writing immediately and keep the thread, because the automated path treats silence as abandonment regardless of who caused it.
- **Cold applications are the working channel (corrected 2026-10-30).** Four of the five interview processes (YinzCam, Speechify, Fanatics, Fetch) came from cold applications submitted through the company portal. PNC is the exception. Do not conclude that warm introductions are required: the constraint is fit-to-stage alignment and response timing, not the absence of a referral.
- **The generic 70-score bulk tier produces nothing.** Playlist, Instacart, SentiLink, Ulta Beauty, Thomson Reuters, Eight Sleep: six rejections, zero interviews. PNC is excluded from this pattern on purpose, because PNC progressed through full interview rounds and ended in an external hiring freeze, which is not a fit rejection.
- **Interview conversion as of 2026-10-30:** 5 processes reached interviews out of 81 tracked applications (about 6%); 19 rejections (23%); 54 still silent in `applied`. The binding constraint is response volume, not application volume.
